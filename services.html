Our services

Northisle Partners helps Canadian small and mid-size businesses add capacity with Philippines-based staff, done-for-you growth services and practical systems. Choose one service or combine several.

  • Offshore staff
  • Lead sourcing
  • Outbound campaigns
  • Social media and video
  • Custom systems
  • Back office
  • AI agent fleet
  • Freelance and team management
👥

Offshore staff

Dedicated Philippines-based team members who work your hours, follow your processes and report on their work every day. We help define the role, onboard the person and keep things running smoothly.

Example tasks

  • Telemarketers for outbound calls and appointment setting
  • Lead generation specialists who research and qualify prospects
  • Customer service agents for phone, email and chat
  • Operations and virtual assistants for admin, data entry and inbox management
  • Order processing, booking and follow-up support
  • Updating your CRM and keeping records tidy
🎯

Done-for-you lead sourcing

We find and organize businesses that match your ideal customer profile, so your sales team spends its time talking to prospects instead of searching for them.

Example tasks

  • Build targeted lists by industry, city or province
  • Find business websites, general emails and phone numbers from public sources
  • Check for duplicates and remove businesses that do not fit
  • Deliver lists as a spreadsheet or load them into your CRM
  • Refresh lists on a regular schedule
📞

Outbound email and calling campaigns

Structured outreach to start conversations with potential customers. We write the messaging, run the campaign and track the results with you.

Example tasks

  • Write cold email sequences and call scripts
  • Send personalized email outreach and follow-ups
  • Make outbound calls to introduce your business
  • Book meetings and appointments into your calendar
  • Log every call and reply so you can see progress
  • Review results and refine the messaging
🎬

Social media and video

Consistent content that keeps your business visible. We handle the planning, writing and posting so your channels stay active.

Example tasks

  • Plan and publish Facebook posts and page updates
  • Share posts in relevant groups and communities
  • Support for your YouTube channel: uploads, titles and descriptions
  • Write video scripts and outlines
  • Design eye-catching thumbnails
  • Keep a simple content calendar
🛠️

Custom systems

Practical software built around how your business actually works, from a simple website to a full internal tool.

Example tasks

  • Business websites and landing pages
  • CRMs for tracking leads, calls and deals
  • Dashboards that show your key numbers at a glance
  • Web and mobile apps for staff or customers
  • Automations that connect the tools you already use
  • Ongoing updates and improvements
📋

Back office

Reliable support for the behind-the-scenes work that keeps a team running, so nothing falls through the cracks.

Example tasks

  • Payroll preparation and timesheet checks
  • HR support: onboarding paperwork and staff records
  • Monitoring staff end-of-day reports and flagging issues
  • Building and maintaining shift schedules
  • Tracking attendance and coverage gaps
  • Weekly summaries for owners and managers
🤖

Managed AI agent fleet

AI agents can take on repetitive digital tasks. We set them up for your workflows and supervise them, with people reviewing the work where it matters.

Example tasks

  • Research prospects and summarize findings
  • Draft follow-up emails for human review
  • Monitor inboxes, forms or data and send alerts
  • Prepare daily and weekly reports
  • Keep spreadsheets and CRMs up to date
  • Combine AI agents with offshore staff for oversight
🤝

Freelance and team management

Work directly with our founder, Bergel Sarenas, on a specific project, or have him manage your remote team day to day.

Example tasks

  • One-off projects: systems, websites or process clean-up
  • Hands-on management of your existing remote staff
  • Setting up processes, scripts and reporting for a new team
  • Hiring support and onboarding for offshore roles
  • Regular check-ins and performance reviews
