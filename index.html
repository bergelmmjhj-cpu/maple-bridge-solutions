An honest look

Why offshore staff from the Philippines?

Offshore staffing is not right for every role, but for many Canadian businesses it is a practical way to add capacity. Here is an honest look at the benefits.

Shifts aligned with
your business hours.From Atlantic to Pacific time, or after hours
  • Strong English communication. English is an official language in the Philippines and is widely used in business and education.
  • Cost-effective capacity. Labour costs in the Philippines are generally lower than in Canada, so your budget can cover more hours of support.
  • Experienced talent pool. The Philippines has a large, established outsourcing industry with many people experienced in calling, customer service and admin work.
  • Coverage across Canadian time zones. Staff can work shifts aligned with your business hours, from Atlantic to Pacific time, or after hours for extended coverage.
  • Managed, not just hired. We pair staff with clear processes, scripts and daily end-of-day reporting so you can see what is getting done.

What does it cost?

A full-time specialist (40 hours a week) starts from CAD $600 every 2 weeks. For a limited time, website development is free when you hire a full-time specialist. Tailored systems and CRMs are custom-quoted, or included when you hire 6 or more staff.

Am I locked into a long contract?

No. Your first month has no contract: pay just that month and walk away if it's not right. After that, simple 6-month terms renew until you cancel.

What if my specialist isn't the right fit?

We replace them free of charge.

Will they work during my business hours?

Yes. Staff work shifts aligned with your business hours, from Atlantic to Pacific time, or after hours if you want extended coverage.

How will I know the work is getting done?

We set up clear processes, scripts and daily end-of-day reports, track output and keep you informed, so you can see exactly what is getting done.

Do I have to handle recruiting, payroll or benefits?

No. There's no recruitment cost and no benefits or payroll overhead on your side. We help define the role, onboard the person and keep things running smoothly.

Who will I actually work with?

You can work directly with our founder, Bergel Sarenas, on a specific project, or have him manage your remote team for you day to day.
