An honest look

Why offshore staff from the Philippines?

Offshore staffing is not right for every role, but for many Canadian businesses it is a practical way to add capacity. Here is an honest look at the benefits.

On the floor

Your team in the Philippines

Dedicated people at their desks, covering the hours your business needs: calls, follow-ups, admin and a daily record of the work.

  • Strong English communication. English is an official language in the Philippines and is widely used in business and education.
  • Cost-effective capacity. Labour costs in the Philippines are generally lower than in Canada, so your budget can cover more hours of support.
  • Experienced talent pool. The Philippines has a large, established outsourcing industry with many people experienced in calling, customer service and admin work.
  • Coverage across Canadian time zones. Staff can work shifts aligned with your business hours, from Atlantic to Pacific time, or after hours for extended coverage.
  • Managed, not just hired. We pair staff with clear processes, scripts and daily end-of-day reporting so you can see what is getting done.
