Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect the personal information you share with us. Last updated November 16, 2026.

Who we are

Northisle Partners provides Philippines-based offshore staff and related services to Canadian businesses. We are responsible for the personal information described here. For any privacy question or request, email hello@northislepartners.org.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email and message, and your company and phone number if you choose to give them.
  • Job applications: your name, email, location, experience, availability, expected pay, links to your CV or introduction, and anything else you write in the form.
  • Discovery call bookings: the details you enter when booking through Cal.com, such as your name, email and preferred time.
  • Emails you send us: whatever you include in them.
  • Basic technical data: like any website, our hosting providers record standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested.

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies on this website. We do not sell personal information.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a discovery call.
  • To provide and manage the services you sign up for.
  • To assess job applicants and contact them about suitable roles.
  • To keep our website secure and working.

We only use your information for these purposes, or for another purpose you agree to.

Who helps us

We use a few trusted service providers to run this website. They handle your information only to provide their service to us:

  • FormSubmit delivers our website forms to our email inbox. Forms may show a brief spam check before sending.
  • Cal.com handles discovery call bookings.
  • GitHub Pages and Netlify host this website.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts this website uses, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.
  • Our email provider stores the messages we send and receive.

Some of these providers, and our own team in the Philippines, may store or access information outside Canada. When that happens, the information may be subject to the laws of those countries.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and client records for as long as we need them to work with you and to meet our legal and accounting obligations. We keep job applications for up to 12 months so we can consider you for future roles, unless you ask us to delete yours sooner. After that, we delete the information.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorized access, and we limit access to the people who need it to do their work.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email hello@northislepartners.org and we will respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. Applicants in the Philippines can also contact the National Privacy Commission.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
