How we work

From first call to a working team.

Three steps, no long forms, and no recruiting on your side. Here's exactly what happens after you book.

Book a discovery call See the terms

Step by step

Three steps. Clear at every one.

You stay in control of the work. We take care of finding, onboarding and managing the people who do it.

  1. 01
    30 minutes

    Book a discovery call

    In 30 minutes we go through the work: the roles, the hours, the tools you use and what a good result looks like for you.

    • Pick a time that suits you on our booking page
    • No long forms to fill in first
  2. 02
    Plan and set-up

    We build your plan and team

    We propose the right mix of offshore staff, services and systems, then set up the people, scripts, processes and reporting.

    • We help define the role and onboard the person
    • No recruitment cost on your side
  3. 03
    Every day after

    Launch, monitor and improve

    Your team starts working. We track daily output with end-of-day reports, keep you informed and adjust as your needs change.

    • Daily end-of-day reports so you can see what is getting done
    • Free replacement if your specialist isn't the right fit

What you get

Managed, not just hired.

Every team comes with the structure that keeps the work visible and on track.

  • Processes and scripts

    Clear processes and scripts set up for your team, so the work is done your way.

  • Daily end-of-day reports

    We track output and keep you informed, so you can see exactly what is getting done.

  • Your business hours

    Shifts aligned with your hours, from Atlantic to Pacific time, or after hours for extended coverage.

The terms

Flexible from day one.

No recruiting, no benefits or payroll overhead, and no contract for your first month. You turn a fixed cost into a flexible one.

03
